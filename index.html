How this tracker works: it averages the latest 60 unique one-second Binance BTCUSDT candle closes. At a five-minute UTC boundary, it locks that rolling average as the round's opening reference. UP means the current rolling average is at or above that opening reference; DOWN means it is below it. Data sources: Binance BTCUSDT is primary. If Binance cannot be reached, Coinbase BTC-USD is sampled once per second as a backup and is clearly labelled. Switching sources resets the sample buffer; the opening reference is established at the next eligible five-minute boundary.
Important: neither spot feed is Polymarket's Chainlink settlement TWAP. This tracker does not guarantee the market outcome.
